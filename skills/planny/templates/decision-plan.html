Decision plan / Booking system / The Old Mill

Choose one booking system before the season opens.

The Old Mill takes bookings by phone and a paper diary. Two rooms were double booked in August. This plan picks one system, tests it for four weeks, and keeps the phone line for people who need it.

Why replace the diary

Not because paper is old. Because two people can write in it at once. Reception takes about 40 calls a week across two shifts. The diary lives at the desk, so a booking made on a Saturday morning is invisible to the person answering on Sunday. In August that produced two double bookings and a refund. The same month, 14 calls went to voicemail and never came back.

Outcome

One calendar that both shifts and the room owners can see. Guests can book online without calling. Deposits are taken at the time of booking.

Why now

The season opens in March and the room owners renew in January. A system chosen in November can be set up and tested before the first busy weekend. Choosing in February means going live during it.

02 Options

Compare three systems on paper

Five rooms, one calendar, deposits. Not a property management system.

In scope

  • One shared calendar for five rooms and two reception shifts.
  • Online booking with a card deposit and an email confirmation.
  • A four-week trial of the top two systems, with the same test bookings.
  • Data export, so leaving the system later does not mean losing the diary.

Out of scope

  • Channel manager integrations with travel sites. Revisit after the first season.
  • A new website. The current site gets a booking button only.
  • Payroll or accounting changes. The accountant keeps the current export format.

Review cadence

  • Wednesday, 20 minutes: Jonah, Sofia and one reception lead on trial notes and blockers.
  • After each trial week: one scorecard page, filled in before the next demo.
  • 20 November: decision meeting with Ellen, then a one-page note to the room owners.
Done means
  • One system, one calendar, both shifts on it.
  • A deposit taken at booking without a manual step.
  • A full diary export that opens in a spreadsheet.
  • Reception can take a booking in under three minutes.
Open questions
