Project plan / Studio two / Fern and Flint

Open a second studio without doubling the kiln bill.

Fern and Flint turns away about 40 people a term. A second room fixes that, but only if one shared kiln, one glaze bench and one extra teacher can carry the load.

02
Why

Why a second room, and why this winter

The waiting list is real demand, not curiosity. The current room is the limit.

Studio one holds eight wheels and one kiln. Classes fill in two days, and 40 people a term go somewhere else. We lose about 9,000 pounds a year in fees we cannot take, before counting the people who never join the list.

Outcome

Two rooms, 14 wheels, 30 students in the first term at studio two. The kiln stays in studio one, so the move adds floor space without a second firing schedule.

Why now

The print works has been empty since June. The landlord is holding it for us until 14 November and wants a three-year term. In January we lose Ines for a term unless her hours change, and she is the only teacher who can run the glaze induction.

03
What

Scope, work and cost

The job is a fit-out and a timetable. It is not a new brand or a shop.

In scope

  • Six wheels, six benches, a glaze bench and storage in studio two.
  • A shared kiln schedule that keeps both rooms on two firings a week.
  • One new teacher, plus Ines moving to a job share.
  • Accessible entry, a sink and a drying rack that fits the room.

Out of scope

  • A second kiln. Revisit only if firings pass three a week for a full term.
  • A retail area. Online sales stay on the current shop.
  • Weekend masterclasses. The timetable has no room until the second term.
