# Acceptable use

planny publishes a plan at a public link. Use it for plans you have the right to
publish. Do not use it to distribute or promote:

- illegal content, malware, phishing, fraud or instructions for serious harm;
- child sexual abuse material, or content that exploits or endangers a child;
- another person's personal data or confidential information without permission;
- harassment, threats, hate, or content that violates someone's privacy or rights;
- spam, deceptive links, or content that evades the rate limits, sanitiser or plan
  content security policy;
- content that interferes with the service or another person's use of it; or
- content that infringes copyright, trade marks or other rights.

We may remove a plan, with or without notice, if we believe it breaks these rules,
the terms of use, the law or the security of the service. We are not obliged to
monitor every plan.

To report a plan, email `frasergriffithsisme@icloud.com` with the plan URL, what is
wrong and enough information to verify the request. Do not send security
vulnerability reports to that inbox; use the private route in `SECURITY.md`.

The hosted service is operated by Fraser Griffiths. If you self-host planny, you set
and enforce the acceptable-use rules for your own instance.
