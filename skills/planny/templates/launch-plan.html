Launch plan / Cargo line / Marlow Bikes

Launch the cargo line with five dealers, not fifty.

Marlow Bikes has one cargo frame and a queue of shops asking to see it. This plan puts two models into five dealers for spring, and holds the wider launch until the first batch proves it can be built on time.

Why a small batch

Dealers are asking for a cargo bike we cannot supply. A small batch answers that without betting the workshop on a warehouse of frames.

The commuter line pays the bills and has no room to grow. Five dealers have asked for a cargo demo since July, and we have sent one borrowed bike around all five. Each asked for stock, not another demo. A 40-bike batch covers those shops and gives us real warranty data before a wider run.

Outcome

Two models in five shops by the first week of March. Every demo bike is sold or returned by April, and we know the build hours per bike before ordering a second batch.

Why now

The frame supplier has a production slot in January and we lose it if we do not commit by 4 December. Spring is when shops plan demo days. Waiting until summer means another season of sending customers to a competitor.

02 Dealers

Sign five dealers for spring

Two models, five shops and a 90-day term. No direct sales and no new website.

In scope

  • Long-tail and short-tail cargo frames, both on the same rear rack.
  • 40 bikes: 30 for stock, 10 for demos and press.
  • Five dealer agreements with a 90-day payment term.
  • One service guide and a spare-parts kit per shop.

Out of scope

  • An e-cargo model. The motor supplier cannot commit to spring.
  • Online direct sales. All first-batch bikes go through dealers.
  • New colours. The two current powder coats only.
