Plan / 2027 / Harbor Coffee Roasters

Turn a weekend bag club into a subscription.

Harbor Coffee Roasters has 180 weekend customers and one espresso machine. This plan grows the bag club into a recurring subscription without opening a second roastery.

Owner: Harbor Coffee Updated: 20 Oct 2026 Horizon: 9 months Status: Draft
TL;DR
  • The bag club has 180 people and no recurring revenue. A four-week subscription at $38 keeps the same roast and adds a second bag.
  • We need 1,200 subscribers by September to cover the second roaster. The beta starts with 200 existing customers.
  • The risk is churn, not demand. Keep the first 90 days small, measure repeat orders, and only then buy capacity.

The bet

Harbor sells 180 bags on weekends. A subscription keeps the same roast and adds a second bag, without a second roastery. The beta is capped at 200 existing customers; the public offer starts only if 60% reorder in the first 90 days.
